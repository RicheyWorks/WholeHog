Measured, not assumed

pre-registered rules, seeded runs, medians
524× → 33×
the cold-scan trigger, fired and closed

Scanning a cold archive via restore cost 524× the raw-read floor. The scan-sidecar ADR answered it the same day: sorted runs ride in every scan-carrying generation, and coldScan runs 33× faster than resurrection.

191%
the preserve-often workaround, priced dead

Checkpointing every 2k ops costs 191% of the churn's own time (~1.4 MB per checkpoint) against a 25% viability bar. Record-granularity as-of stays held on its consumer trigger — knowing exactly what the fallback costs.

B* ≈ 128k
the amortization frontier (ADR-018)

An EWS-triggered morpher raced against best-fixed across regime block lengths: 2.24× worse at 2k ops, monotone to a ~1% win at 256k — giving ADR-012's re-arming trigger #1 its number.

The findings ledger

discoveries the organism forced upstream
#1

Twine's sink seam

Batches over an indexed store must fan out through the indexes — Twine gained PutSink/DeleteSink at design time, the seam every later routing decision reused.

#2

The wire was read-only — resolved

Deliberately unsolved until a consumer arrived. SmokeSignal gained the WriteRoute seam; a wire client is now a first-class writer whose puts reach every secondary, view, replica, and invalidation.

#3

The watcher wanted to be an organ

The bare tail counter that proved four-subscriber convergence generalized into Rub, engine 13 — a meter fused with a gauge, gaps reported rather than hidden.

#4

The write path wanted to be fault-injectable

Finding #1 earned its keep: Sizzle, engine 14, wraps the sink seam, and composed crash-atomicity is demonstrated at every crash point rather than asserted in a comment.

#5

Curing history needed no scratch copy

Two copies and a recovery pass, to read immutable CRC'd bytes — pure ceremony. DryAge named generationPath for read-only archival consumers, and the dance is gone.

#6

Batches wanted to cross the wire whole

SmokeSignal gained OP_BATCH + BatchRoute — the server reads the whole batch before touching the route, the organism ties the route to Twine, and any wire client gets crash-atomic multi-key batches without knowing Twine exists.
